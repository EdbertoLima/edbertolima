## Edberto Moura Lima

Senior Researcher at [BAW Research](https://www.baw.at/baw-research.html) (Federal Agency for Water Management, Austria), working on **ecohydrology and remote sensing of evapotranspiration**.

**Current work:** [VeSchN](https://www.baw.at/baw-research/projekte/veschn.html) - evapotranspiration of the reed belt at Lake Neusiedl (Sentinel-2, OPTRAM-ET, Google Earth Engine).

### Tools I build

- [**Rainfall Erosivity Monitor**](https://github.com/EdbertoLima/rainfall-erosivity-monitor) - EI30 rainfall erosivity and soil-erosion alerts from GeoSphere Austria station data (Python, Streamlit)
- [**QField erosion survey**](https://github.com/EdbertoLima/erosion-survey-qfield) - field mapping of soil erosion by water following DWA-M 921 (QGIS, QField)

### Research interests

Remote sensing of evapotranspiration and wetland hydrology - hydrological and water quality modelling (SWAT, APEX) - soil management, land-use change and climate adaptation - nature-based solutions

**Stack:** R, Python, SQL, Google Earth Engine, QGIS, SWAT/APEX
